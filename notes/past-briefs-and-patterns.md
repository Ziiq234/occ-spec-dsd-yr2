## Patterns — what to be ready to build
Every single brief asks for **accessibility**. Most also need some mix of:

1. **User accounts with roles** — register, log in, profiles, different access levels (customer / staff / admin).
2. **Booking or scheduling** — consultations, installations, jobs, sessions; avoiding clashes.
3. **A calculator or tracker** — carbon footprint, energy usage, health metrics, progress, job metrics.
4. **A dashboard with charts** — air quality, progress, team performance.
5. **Content / knowledge base** — articles, advice, resources, filtering and search.
6. **Accessibility panel** — font size, contrast/colour schemes, dyslexia-friendly font, alt text, keyboard navigation, saved per user.
7. **Possibly an external API** — weather, air quality, energy data.
8. **Legal pages** — cookie notice, privacy policy, T&Cs, GDPR consent.

Practising these as small reusable skills before March means the real brief becomes "which of these, for this client".
